The Purchase Order screen is the formal procurement transaction used by the Spinning Division's Purchase team to raise a Purchase Order to a raw cotton supplier. A buyer creates a PO either in response to a Purchase Requisition (PR/Indent) from planning, or as a direct spot purchase.

WHO USES IT:
Purchase Department buyers and executives in the Spinning Division.
WHEN:
After a PR is raised by planning, or for a spot/direct market purchase.
WHAT IT PRODUCES:
A numbered Purchase Order that is the mandatory reference for all downstream processing — goods arrival, quality inspection, inventory receipt, and supplier invoice matching. Without a valid PO, goods cannot be received.

BUSINESS SIGNIFICANCE: This is the highest-stakes entry screen in the Raw Material module. Every downstream financial transaction — GST input credit, supplier payment, accounts payable journal — traces back to this PO. Errors in rate, GST treatment, or supplier details create cascading problems in Accounts Payable that are difficult to correct after goods arrive.

Section 5 — Business Logic and Auto-Fill Rules
5.1 GST Determination (On Supplier Select)
Foreign supplier (STATE_FLAG='F')IGSTFLG='N' — No GST. No GSTSTATECODE or GSTINNO check.Registered domestic (SuppType='R')GSTSTATECODE must not be '0'; GSTINNO must not be '0'. Compare states: same=IGSTFLG='Y' (CGST+SGST); diff=IGSTFLG='N' (IGST).Unregistered domesticGSTSTATECODE must not be '0'; GSTINNO not required. State comparison sets IGSTFLG.
5.2 GST Rate Auto-Fill (Col 6 Exit, wosample='N', PRBased='Y')
Same state (IGSTFLG='Y')Grid CGST% = rm_var.CGSTPER; Grid SGST% = rm_var.SGSTPER; Grid IGST% = 0Different state (IGSTFLG='N')Grid CGST% = 0; Grid SGST% = 0; Grid IGST% = rm_var.IGSTPERAll pathsGrid HSN = rm_var.HSN (mandatory — else LV-16); Grid Tax Code = rm_var.TaxCode (mandatory — else LV-17)
